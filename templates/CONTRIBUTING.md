# Contributing

The contributor terms live in ONE place:
<https://github.com/bitbaum/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md>.

GitHub shows that file on the pull-request form of every bitbaum repository
that has no CONTRIBUTING.md of its own, so the right move for a repo is to
have none — not to copy this. A repo with genuinely different terms writes
its own and sets `REQUIRE_DCO=0` on its sweep.

What the terms say, in one line: an outside commit's `Signed-off-by` certifies
the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 and grants the maintainer a licence
that keeps relicensing possible. The auto-merge sweep enforces it.
